# NoSub Cam App Store package

This folder contains the working material for an App Store submission. It is not
the public legal website. Before release, add the remaining review phone number,
obtain legal review where appropriate, and publish the approved
privacy, terms and support pages at stable HTTPS URLs.

Files:

- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`: English privacy policy draft.
- `TERMS_OF_USE.md`: English supplemental terms draft.
- `SUPPORT.md`: content for the required support page.
- `APP_STORE_METADATA.md`: proposed App Store listing fields and privacy answers.
- `REVIEW_NOTES.md`: instructions for App Review.
- `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`: completed, pending and owner-decision items.

English is the only supported language for the initial release. Apple supplies its Standard EULA by default;
the supplemental terms here do not replace that EULA unless a custom license is
entered in App Store Connect.

The App Store workflow lives in `fastlane/`. The editable app icon is in the
Figma file “NoSub Cam — App Icon”; its 1024px export is included in the Xcode
asset catalog.
