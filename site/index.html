01 / Signal path

Direct by design.

NoSub Cam sends the image to the receiver you choose. The publisher does not operate a video relay, user account system, or recording service.

01

Open NoSub Studio

Choose Wi-Fi or Direct USB on your Mac or Windows PC and start the local receiver.

02

Scan once. Remember the link.

Show the pairing QR in NoSub Studio and scan it on iPhone. Both apps remember the pairing; reset it whenever you choose.

03

Use NoSub Camera

Install and approve the desktop camera component, enable output, then select NoSub Camera in your video app. OBS is optional.

02 / Mobile camera

Focused before the first frame.

04 / Capabilities

Local tools, not a service.

The product is intentionally small: two companion apps that connect devices you already own.

Wi-Fi or Direct USB

Use a trusted local Wi-Fi network or connect through Apple's local USB device service.

Camera controls and presets

Select front or rear, resolution, and frame rate from the desktop. Adjust supported exposure, focus, white balance, and zoom, and save presets for next time.

Native webcam output

Select NoSub Camera in your video app for 1080p30 output, without OBS. Approve the extension on Mac or install the camera component on Windows.

No account or tracking

No developer-operated backend, advertising network, analytics SDK, or subscription system.

Security note

Wi-Fi mode uses unencrypted HTTP on your local network. Use a private network you trust, protect pairing details, and never expose the receiver port to the public internet.
