Include your iPhone model, iOS version, computer operating system, connection mode, and exact error message. Do not send pairing keys, private stream URLs, camera footage, or other sensitive content.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
  • macOS or Windows computer running NoSub Studio.
  • For Wi-Fi: both devices on the same trusted private network.
  • For Direct USB: a data-capable cable, unlocked and trusted iPhone, and Apple's USB device service.
  • OBS Studio is currently required when the stream must appear as a virtual webcam in another application.

Quick setup

  1. Install and open NoSub Studio on the computer.
  2. Select Wi-Fi or Direct USB.
  3. Enter the displayed receiver address and pairing key in NoSub Cam.
  4. Grant camera and local-network permission when requested.
  5. Tap Start streaming and keep NoSub Cam in the foreground.

The iPhone cannot connect over Wi-Fi

Confirm both devices are on the same private Wi-Fi, Local Network permission is enabled for NoSub Cam, and the computer firewall allows NoSub Studio. Do not use 127.0.0.1 as the receiver address on the iPhone.

Direct USB does not list the iPhone

Unlock the iPhone, reconnect a data-capable cable, accept the Trust prompt, and refresh devices in NoSub Studio. Windows requires the Apple Mobile Device driver/service. macOS supplies the required service.

Camera or Local Network permission was denied

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, then enable NoSub Cam under Camera and Local Network.

Safe diagnostics

Pairing keys and OBS stream URLs grant access to the current local receiver session. Treat them as private. Restarting the receiver rotates the key.

Network

Wi-Fi video is not encrypted. Use only a trusted local network and never forward the receiver port from your router.
